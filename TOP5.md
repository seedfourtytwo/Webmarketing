# Practice shortlist: top 5 (Millau area)

Selection rules: owner-reachable small business, not agency-built, real pain visible on the site (or no working site), and a job that pays back quickly. Evidence comes from phone/desktop screenshots, measured signals, Google Places data and manual checks. Contact details live in the gitignored `data/contacts_private.csv`.

Quick price frame (to be tested, not a promise): one-page or 4-5 page rebuild 200-400 EUR, optional hosting/maintenance 10-20 EUR/month. Rebuilds are cheap for us; the value is in a fast, visible before/after.

## 1. Aveyron Couverture (roofer, Millau)
- Why first: highest ticket per customer (roof jobs), owner-run, already has two strong phone CTAs (one emergency line), 1982 heritage, so the pitch is a quick fix, not a lecture.
- Seen: raw `[metaslider id="63"]` code printed under the header, huge thin 5-line heading on phone, big empty grey zones. Only 3 Google reviews, so review collection is an add-on.
- Build: 1 page + services (Velux, zinguerie, dépannage) with tap-to-call and a local-SEO title ("Couvreur à Millau").
- Hooks (FR): le code technique visible en haut de page | vos deux numéros sont bien visibles, c'est un vrai plus | les zones grises vides sur mobile.
- Risk: WordPress/Divi site may be maintained by a relative; ask who edits it.

## 2. Katia Angles Vaissettes (independent estate agent)
- Why: solo agent, 152 Google reviews (4.0), website confirmed down by manual check, commissions are large, so a site pays back with one sale.
- Build: personal brand page with listings link and reviews block.
- Hooks (FR): votre site ne s'ouvre plus | vos 152 avis Google ne renvoient vers aucune page | on peut le remettre en ligne vite.
- Risk (important): she may be tied to a network that supplies/blocks personal sites. Check before pitching.

## 3. Boyer Paysagiste (GAEC Boyer, landscaper/nursery)
- Why: medium-size business, 24 Google reviews (4.6). Their site returns HTTP 500 with an empty page on every try and exposes PHP 5.6.
- Build: seasonal page (jardin, plantes, entretien), contact form, opening hours.
- Hooks (FR): votre site affiche une erreur serveur | la version logicielle de l'hébergement est très ancienne | pour la saison de printemps il vaut mieux être prêt tôt.
- Risk: confirm on a phone that it is really down for French visitors before claiming it.

## 4. Les Falaises (chambres d'hôtes, Mostuéjouls) and 5. Domaine de la Cardabelle (chambres d'hôtes, Rivière-sur-Tarn)
- Why: two B&Bs with excellent reviews (46 at 5.0 and 105 at 4.8) and very dated sites (early-2000s frames, GIF menus, shrunk on phones, menu clipped). One build gives a reusable B&B template for both, plus the whole Gorges du Tarn niche.
- Build: gallery, rooms/prices, availability contact, link to the booking channels they already use (we keep Booking/Gîtes de France; the site adds presence and SEO, not a replacement).
- Hooks (FR): sur mobile la page s'affiche en miniature | le menu est coupé au bord de l'écran | vos photos méritent un bouton de réservation visible.
- Risk: lodging owners may be tech-shy and seasonal; email address on Les Falaises is a wanadoo.fr one, so a call or visit may work better than email.

## Alternates
- **LC2B** (timber-frame builder, La Cavalerie): decent site but garbled hero text, no phone above the fold, 11 s load. Pitch is speed + conversion, not a rebuild. Sector was mislabeled "accountant" in earlier data.
- **Caumes des Livres** (bookshop, 210 reviews, large Facebook following, no own site) and **Institut Abelia Beauté** (64 reviews, Planity only): cheap wins, small budgets.
- **Hôtel des Voyageurs** (350 reviews): listed domain no longer resolves, but lodging likely relies on Booking by choice.
